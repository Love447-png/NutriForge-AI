# Child Nutrition Basics

Children under five need meal frequency, dietary diversity, and energy density.

Key principles:
- Offer 3 meals and 2 snacks each day after 6 months of age.
- Every meal should try to include one staple, one protein source, and one fruit or vegetable.
- Add a small spoon of oil, ghee, or groundnut paste to soft foods to raise calories without increasing meal size too much.
- Continue breastfeeding where applicable.
- During illness, feed smaller but more frequent meals and continue fluids.
- After illness, add one extra meal each day for around two weeks to support catch-up growth.

Warning signs needing referral:
- Edema or swelling
- Lethargy or reduced responsiveness
- Very poor appetite
- Rapid weight loss
- Persistent diarrhea or fever
