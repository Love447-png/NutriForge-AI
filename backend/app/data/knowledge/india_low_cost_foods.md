# India Low-Cost Foods By State

Affordable child-friendly foods vary by region, but most Indian states can rely on a similar pattern: cereal plus pulse plus one protective food plus one energy booster.

North India:
- Wheat roti, bajra roti, seasonal saag, roasted chana, curd, banana, jaggery, groundnut.

East India:
- Rice, masoor dal, egg curry, pumpkin, sweet potato, leafy greens, fish where available.

West India:
- Jowar, bajra, matki, moong, peanut chutney, curd rice, sesame laddoo, banana.

South India:
- Ragi malt, idli, dosa with sambar, curd rice, green gram, egg, red rice, nendran banana.

General low-cost swaps:
- Egg can be replaced by roasted chana, soy nuggets, or groundnut-sesame powder.
- Milk can be replaced by curd or diluted curd-based porridges when tolerated.
- Ghee is helpful but oil can also increase energy density in porridge or khichdi.
