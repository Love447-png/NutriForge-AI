# WHO Simplified Growth Rules

This system uses approximate WHO-inspired screening logic rather than formal z-scores.

Approximate community screening:
- Weight well below age reference suggests underweight.
- Height well below age reference suggests stunting or chronic growth lag.
- Low body mass for height can suggest wasting or recent undernutrition.
- Multiple low signals together increase clinical concern.

Follow-up logic:
- Healthy means no major red flags but continue monitoring monthly.
- Needs Attention means one or more moderate concerns and should trigger diet strengthening plus closer re-check.
- High Risk means multiple strong concerns or visible clinical danger signs and should trigger urgent referral.
